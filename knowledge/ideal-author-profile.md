# Ideal Author Profile (template)

## Must-haves
- An audience they can bring (newsletter, social following, community)
- An active way of promoting their own work
- Contact information that can be found

## Segments
| Segment | Audience size | Sender | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Big names | 100K+ | Founder | Conversation |
| Mid-tier | 10K–100K | Scout | "Worth a 5-minute look?" |
| Emerging | <10K | Scout | Link to `{product_url}` |
| Author-preneurs / coaches | any | Founder or Scout | Partnership |

## Scoring
Score each prospect 1–10 on audience, engagement, fit with the product's strengths, and intent signals. Define what a 9–10, 6–8 and 1–5 look like for your product.

## Intent signals
Public posts or behaviours that suggest an author would be receptive (e.g. frustration with their current platform, interest in selling direct).

## Exclusions
Who never to contact, and why.
