# Email Templates (template)

Templates are starting points. Scout always loosens them up and adds something specific about the author.

## Signature rules
- Founder emails: use the signature in `founder.md`
- Scout emails: `{Product}` only, no personal names (until scout personas are defined)

## Sequence shape
| Email | Day | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | Specific compliment + one clear reason to look |
| 2 | 3 | One concrete benefit, with proof |
| 3 | 7 | Why the product exists (founder story) |
| 4 | 12 | Answer the most likely objection |
| 5 | 18 | Social proof |
| 6 | 25 | Low-pressure check-in |
| 7 | 35 | Polite close |
